# After Wheaton

This repository is being turned into **After Wheaton**, an unofficial iOS app for people living life after Wheaton. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Wheaton College.

It's a work in progress and will keep the SAGA (Anderson Commons) menu, remove every feature that needed a Wheaton login, and repurposes Who's Who as a directory of trustees, donors, faculty, and administrators. See [TODO.md](TODO.md) for the full plan.

## Credits

After Wheaton is built on the original Wheaton College iOS app, created by **Chris Anderson** and **Drew Hannay** ([pushchris/wc-ios](https://github.com/pushchris/wc-ios)). The git history credits other contributors as well.

## Building

Open `Wheaton App.xcodeproj` in Xcode 26 or later, choose your team under Signing & Capabilities, and run the "Wheaton App" scheme on an iOS 17+ simulator or device. There are no third-party dependencies to install. Remaining modernization work is tracked in [TODO.md](TODO.md).
